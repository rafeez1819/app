Five things SHERIN will not do

Most assistants are shared, remote and opaque. SHERIN is the opposite.

Depend on the cloud
Everything runs on your hardware. If the network disappears, SHERIN does not notice.
Carry heavy payloads
Only small reference IDs travel through the reasoning pipeline, never the content itself. That is why answers arrive in milliseconds.
Guess
It is deterministic: the same question takes the same path, and every answer traces back to a knowledge unit.
Serve anyone else
One model, one person. It reads your tone and mood, and your emotional data stays yours.
Stand still
The roadmap includes a star-teacher model that upgrades SHERIN over time, so the system keeps growing with its owner.

How a question travels

SHERIN's memory is a sphere of 30 layers, each divided into 12 sectors. Every sector is an Area with its own entry and exit.

  1. 1

    Classify

    The content of the question decides its Area. Location never does.

  2. 2

    Route

    The outer ring carries the request to that Area's nearest port.

  3. 3

    Process

    The Area's capsule reasons over its own knowledge units.

  4. 4

    Return

    The inner ring, turning the opposite way, carries the answer out.

Data processed inside an Area never spills into another.
